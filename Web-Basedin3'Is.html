ABOUT THE PRODUCT

What is Atsara?
Atsara is a Filipino pickled papaya relish. Sweet, sour, and crunchy - perfect partner for lechon, fried chicken, barbecue, and grilled dishes.

Our Flavor:
✅ Sweet - Original classic flavor

Ingredients:
Fresh green papaya, carrots, bell pepper, onion, garlic, ginger, vinegar, sugar, and spices.

Why choose Aling Remy's Atsara?
✅ 100% Homemade
✅ Fresh ingredients from local market
✅ No preservatives
✅ Made with love by Aling Remy
